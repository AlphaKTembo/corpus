Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Corpus ("we", "us") provides training and health dashboards and AI coaching features. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the controls you have. Corpus is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

What We Collect

  • Account information from sign-in (Google or Apple), such as email and a stable account id.
  • Connected service data you authorize (for example intervals.icu or FitnessSyncer): activities, vitals, sleep, body metrics, and related training metrics.
  • Medical and other documents you upload for summaries and reference.
  • Text and voice inputs you submit to coaching or chat features, used to answer your requests in your account context.
  • Usage and diagnostic logs needed to keep the service reliable and secure.

How We Use Data

  • To render your dashboards, analytics, and coaching surfaces.
  • To generate AI summaries and insights you request, using your connected data and notes as context.
  • To maintain reliability, security, and performance.

Sharing

Your data is not sold. Data is only shared with third-party providers you explicitly connect (such as intervals.icu or FitnessSyncer), with infrastructure and AI providers required to deliver requested features, or when required by law.

AI and Service Providers

To provide AI coaching, chat, and summaries, the relevant parts of your data (such as training and recovery metrics, the text of uploaded medical documents, and your messages) are sent to the following providers when you use those features:

  • Anthropic (Claude): coaching briefs, training plans, workout debriefs, chat, and medical document summaries.
  • OpenAI (Whisper): transcription of voice messages you record.
  • Google Firebase: sign-in and account authentication.

These providers process data on our behalf under their commercial API terms, which do not allow them to use it to train their models. Corpus does not send your data to AI providers for any purpose other than answering your requests.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No system is 100% secure, so please use strong credentials and keep your access private.

Data Retention

Data is retained as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services you use.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account in the Corpus iOS app under More → Delete Account. That request removes your Corpus account record and associated server-side data for the product. You may also email support@kurasika.tech to request deletion.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact support@kurasika.tech.
